# 🎓 Campus Market

> A student-to-student marketplace for college essentials — books, lab coats, calculators, and more.

**Campus Market** connects **seniors** who no longer need their academic supplies with **juniors** who need them. Every listing can be offered for **sale**, **rent**, or **both**, giving students flexible, affordable options without leaving campus.

---

## ✨ Features

- 📚 **Books by Semester & Subject** — Juniors filter listings precisely to their current semester and syllabus.
- 🧪 **Accessories Too** — Lab coats, calculators, drawing instruments, lab records, and other college essentials.
- 💰 **Buy or Rent** — A single listing can offer both options. The seller decides.
- 🔒 **Verified Campus Community** — Only students can join, keeping the marketplace safe and relevant.
- 🔍 **Powerful Filters** — Category, semester, subject, and price.
- 📩 **Built-in Request Flow** — Juniors send buy/rent requests; seniors accept or decline.
